# Krafft Ehricke on the dilation of the mind
>
>"Man's mind and spirit grow with the space in which they are allowed to operate"
>- Krafft A. Ehricke

The limits you place on your mind is the potential with which you will actuate on. The keyword here is "allowed". You have to choose to constantly push yourself further. To try harder things. To forgo the comfortable routine in favor of the uncomfortable new experiences. Be careful not to cap your self-actualization by placing perceived limits onto yourself.

The mind will expand and from that new expanse will come even more expansion if you allow your newly dilated mind to grow by actively seeking that growth.
