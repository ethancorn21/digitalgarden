Once potential energy is actuated into kinetic energy, it is much easier to maintain. Think about how easy going to the gym is if you have gone the previous 3 months straight. Now think about how hard it is if you haven't been in 2 weeks. Our natural state will always trend toward potential energy. So if you stop adding energy to the system, it will stall. You must keep the energy going even if it is only a little bit. You must stoke the flame to keep the embers burning.
