# Complex Systems Needs to Be Designed For the Lowest Base Denominator

Complex systems are rarely designed, instead they are formed over years of quick fixes and convoluted implementation of new pieces into existing infrastructure. The problem is that every system eventually becomes a rats maze of confusing and idiotic deployments. 

New hires or users into a system are then forced to sink or swim. You either learn or don't use it by going around the system. People that can't adapt to dog shit systems, even if they would be perfect for the job any other time, fail at their job.

Of course this is assuming that complex systems produce more results than simpler ones. They don't. Keep the complexity away from the moving parts and more towards the dynamic malleable ones such as humans. Processes should be damn near automated to their fullest extent. This allows for flexible mechanisms (people), to focus entirely on the dynamic rather than the menial.

This is the hope of AI and AI augmented systems. Of course though, implementation of AI into current systems seems to only increase the sloppiness of the system.
