# Unique Ability For Culture To Overpower Instinct

Humans have the unique ability for culture to overpower their instinctual programming. Instinctually we desire reproduction, survival, and thriving. Yet we have priests and monks who take the vow and cast aside romantic connection and reproduction.

Humans are the only animal that can do this. A Zebra won't forgo mating opportunity on the basis of moralistic notions.
