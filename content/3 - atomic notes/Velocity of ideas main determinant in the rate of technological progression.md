# Velocity of Ideas

How quickly ideas can proliferate and survive implementation testing determines the baseline advancement of the civilization. The golden truth lies in the middle between conservative and instant adoption of new ideas and technology. How can we quickly stress test new technologies to determine where advancement must take place where factor of it's utility > the measurement of its setbacks?

This is where the velocity of ideas is important. QC through mass "tryouts" acts as a stress tester. Is new ideas can quickly proliferate and be experimented with by the masses. Product evolution is expedited. 

Look at the business model of LLM companies. They allow free tiers, which allow users to experiment and see if they want to pay for the service, and LLM companies get training data and user analytics.
