# Group think

Group Think is the collective turning off of the feedback loops that allow humans to persist through differing opinions and differing resources. By having a feedback loop, such as the collective consciousness focusing on one thing the only way forward is the one idea, the one shared idea of the group.

In a normal healthy group that is resistant to group think the feedback loop that stops group think from forming are the dissenters of that group. 

What then causes the feedback loop of a normal group to breakdown into that of one compromised to group think?
