# Freeing of Io in Greek Mythology

![[Peter_Paul_Rubens_-_Mercury_and_Argos,_1636-1638 copy.jpg]]

Io, a nymphal priestess of Zeus's wife Hera, became the object of the Zeus's desires. But weary of Hera learning of his affair, Zeus turned Io into a white heifer. Though Hera saw through this and demanded the white heifer as a gift, and because it was only a cow, Zeus could not refuse her request. 

Hera charged Argus The One Hundred Eyed to watch over the newly gifted Io as Argus could always keep at least one watchful eye on Io, even in sleep.

Zeus, not falter by Hera's attempts to stop the adultery dispatched Hermes to free Io under the guardsmen's watch. Using music and stories, Hermes was able to lull the giant to sleep with riddles and music. Upon slaying him, Io was freed.
