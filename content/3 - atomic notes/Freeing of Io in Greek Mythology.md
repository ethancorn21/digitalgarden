# Freeing of Io in Greek Mythology

![[Peter_Paul_Rubens_-_Mercury_and_Argos,_1636-1638 copy.jpg]]

Io, a nymphal priestess of Zeus's wife Hera, became the object of the king of the god's desire. Upon learning this, Io was turned into a white heifer in order to hide her identity. Argus could always keep one eye on Io. Hermes was dispatched to free Io under the guardsmen's watch. Using music and stories, Hermes was able to lull the giant to sleep, all one hundred eyes of his. Upon slaying him, Zeus freed Io.
