# Life Pours From You or On You

A life well lived is a consequence of what you do while living. If you wish to die on your deathbed with no regrets you must put yourself in positions where you can't give yourself the option to regret. When faced with the choice of the uncomfortable or the comfortable, why not choose the uncomfortable? Really, what do you have to lose but self-satisfaction of serving the hedonistic mind?

If you wish to have stability, family, healthy relationships. These cannot be formed with comfortability. At some point in your journey of attainment, you will have to have grit and push on. The loftier your goals, the more you will have to grit. It's an inherent feature of the system. It will require exponential effort to achieve linear gains.

It's from uncomfortable things that you grow of course. Doing the things you normally wouldn't do. You know how corny it was to crack open a book for the first time and just read it for no reason. Sitting there thinking, "who do you think you are". But fuck me if it doesn't work.
