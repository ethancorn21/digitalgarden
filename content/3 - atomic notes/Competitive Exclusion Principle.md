# Competitive Exclusion Principle

If a self-reinforcing feedback loop rewards the winner of a competition with the means to win further competitions, the result will be the elimination of all but a few competitors.

Mechanism by which late-stage capitalism forces conglomeration into a few truly powerful corporations. We try to fight this with anti-trust acts and incentives for small business and competition.
