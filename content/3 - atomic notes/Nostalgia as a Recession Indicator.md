# Nostalgia as a Recession Indicator

When the past starts seeming more glamorous than the future, we can extrapolate that where we are is not perceptually better than where we were. This could be from variety of factors, but when a larger share of society starts becoming nostalgic and thinking of better times in the past, we can infer that what is affecting everyone has to be of factors that do in fact effect everyone. The most reasonable suspect is the economy. The second most reasonable suspect is the way of living is not coherent for a prosperous people. Again, this sort of draws back to the economy, so I would reasonably say that a nostalgic index of the broader population would be a good economic recession indicator.
