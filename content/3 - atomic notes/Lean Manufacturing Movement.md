# Lean Manufacturing Movement

Two core tenets of Lean:
- time taken to convert raw materials into manufactured products is the best indicator of quality, customer happiness, and employee happiness
- the best predictor of short lead times is small batches of work

So shorten lead time by shrinking the size of batches. 

Lean's main goal is to create constant movement through a system through flow and pull, rather than force and push. 

This also helps maintain quality, because more consistent quality = less push down the reverse supply chain.
