# Efficiency Multiplication Since Using Claude Code
![[IMG_3042.jpeg|400]]

Claude Code allows me to try more things. What would have once been outside of my scope because of a lack of knowledge or skills is within experimentation range. 

You see many people think that the trade off becomes, "Do I want to get more done with less retention, or be able to experiment more at a higher level yet not truly understand the underlying structure". Yet you can purposefully use AI to experiment more, learn more, and retain more. The key is in not asking for answer, but instead asking for teaching. Understanding how and why, not just the what.

A good example of this is your homelab docs. Allowing AI to generate the guide for how to do it, gives the the opportunity to work through homelab deployment yourself, yet not be completely lost and stuck on the smallest steps. It allows you to experience more failures and not be stuck on just a few.

I find that for more technical acts I would like to rely on Claude/AI in general. It's not going anywhere so understanding and creating the mental frameworks for how to best guide AI through prompts and context is one of the best skills to have. The AI integration factor will only become greater as tokens are commoditized. 

I see people bank on the fact that we will eventually need "the great lost art of manually troubleshooting". I personally think its BS. In my experience, allowing AI to troubleshoot, gives me a broader understanding of the system, its limits and capabilities, in a short time span than if I was painstakingly digging through code and configurations.

Again, back to this idea of a golden mean. The future will be dominated by those that can use AI at the appropriate time. Meaning that non-AI adopters are 100% cooked and will be relegated to the trenches. But the vibe coders who produce only slop will also be relegated to short stints of insane productivity followed by deciding to burn the house down instead of fixing the slop of a foundation it was built on.

So intelligent use and architecture of AI created systems is needed. It becomes less being a keyboard jockey and more thinking through the why's that a glorified text prediction algorithm can't see because of the context that it was not given.

All of this is to say, use Claude Code or any LLM in the CLI. Get good with it. Understand the limitations and strengths the tools. Utilize them to their fullest extent, capitalizing on the good and using Human power to compensate for the bad.
