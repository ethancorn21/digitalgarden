# Applying 5 S's To Daily Life

You can apply the 5 S's to eliminate waste by:

Taking the time to organize and standardize your tools. You Should never be searching for a tool. For example, when using obsidian notes, your system should present the ideas as you need them, not having to dig for the tools you need is the foundation for efficiency.

Analyze your methods. Are you using the best tools for the job? When you are studying for certifications or classes, even work, are you using the best tools? Can you incorporate Anki cards? Can you write about it in your own words?

Clean daily. Make sure your work station is clean and tidy. Cleanliness is next to godliness.

Standardize. Variations lead to energy spent adapting. This is energy spent not in motion.

Review work. How can you improve the work you are doing? Do you need a need process / tool? Is your energy spent in the right place?
