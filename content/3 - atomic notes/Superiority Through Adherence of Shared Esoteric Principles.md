# Superiority Through Adherence of Esoteric Shared Principles

Humans can create feelings of status and superiority by following a shared set of esoteric principles that are different than the norm. 

We are simultaneously status seeking creatures and creatures that want to expense the least amount of energy seeking that status, and so we try to get the highest ROI of our energy spent to status gained.

When self improvement becomes the public identity, when it becomes the metric to seek and best others at, we inevitably see the rise of performative self improvement. Or performative discipline needed in order to improve the self.

Consumption of meaninglessness meant to sell you the *feeling* like you did something. But did you?

These activities are all easy to do because they allow for some aspect of social posturing, flexing. They have a goal. They have reputation for difficulty in society's eyes. These attributes allow them to be self sustaining to the participants because the true goal is to progress to show off to others how great you are. 

It's arbitrary hardship. Suffering for the sake of suffering because much of the current world is too easy to live in. 

So a cult of the disciplined is formed. Its main goal is to draw participants into a pissing contest for who can create the most unnecessary hardship in their own life so that they can claim the title of the most disciplined. Status seeking creatures living in a world of utmost ease will crucify themselves in order to grain status.
