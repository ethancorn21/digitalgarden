# Customer Acquisition Cost vs Lifetime Value

Cost to acquire a customer versus the amount of value that customer will give you over the course of their purchasing life.

$1000 to acquire one customer seems like a lot, but if you are selling hundreds of thousands of dollars worth of products net gain.

Books are fucking expensive. Reading them is expensive. But the potential for gain is so great that you would be an idiot not to at least invest in yourself a little.
