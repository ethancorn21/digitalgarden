# Ever Moving Goal Post Of Satisfaction

There is the idea that satisfaction is more permanent than happiness. That instead of seeking to find happiness, we should instead create the circumstances for satisfaction. 

What you have noticed, and maybe its a consequence of being a naive 20 year old, is that what satisfied me once does not carry the same effect as it once had. As you live your life and experience more, the things with which you found satisfaction become lesser in their potency, in the same sense that an expanding room makes the things inside appear smaller.

If we assume that the trend will continue, as time goes on and you experience more, you will inevitably fall down this throttling of satisfaction response. I guess the better idea is to see the overarching narratives that you formed in pursuit of satisfaction, what are the things that generally bring you long term contentment versus short burst of happiness?
