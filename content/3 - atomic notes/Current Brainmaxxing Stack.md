# Current Brainmaxxing Stack

Training the brain is as much about the environment in which it exists as feeding it constant, yet controlled inputs. Create your learning system and stick to it. Compounded over years, the mind will expand to fill the space allotted to it.

## Foundational stuff:

Get good sleep. Follow a predetermined shutdown ritual. Right now, reading fiction an hour before bed seems to be working well to lower the amount of time it takes you to fall asleep from when your eyes close. Sleep with earplugs in, curtains drawn. Use your bed only for sleep, not relaxing.

Physically exert yourself. Workout hard and run for a long time. The mind is a byproduct of the body. If you can't maintain your body, your mind will not grow. Plus, what is the point of being a pencil necked nerd.

Consistency. If you only do when you feel like it, you will never push past the valley of despair. Build habits where necessary.

Iterate on processes after a set trial period to ensure that your methods are continually being refined.

## inputs:

Variety of inputs. If you spend your day learning from YouTube that is a day spent not learning from books. If you spend the day reading, that is a day immersed in potentially dated knowledge. Find balance between the two but understand that passive social media consumption is a lot easier than passive consumption of literature.

Mix it up. Read different genres about different subjects in different forms of media. You have been enjoying: listening to modern fiction audiobooks, reading physical classic literature, note taking non-fiction in the day, and pairing technical books with projects where you implement the material into a real world situation.

Deep dive into stuff you find interesting. Fall down the wikipedia rabbit hole. Just take notes on the stuff you think is cool.

Remembering the inputs. Take notes and use Anki for the stuff you want to remember. Connect new knowledge to existing knowledge through novel connections.

Strategic use of chemical stimulants. Don't abuse caffeine, use it as a tool. Don't drink it on the weekends, unless you have work that needs to be done. Allow your tolerance to diminish so that when you need caffeine, it's efficacy is insane.

## outputs:

Being able to conceptualize knowledge is one piece of the puzzle. If you wish to exert it onto the world, you must also be able to act upon learned wisdom. Most often this will come as speaking. This is different from social communication, as most social communication should be listening, but when you speak you must be articulate. 

Write and write often. It does not matter about what, just that you write and force your thoughts, which are incomplete and incomprehensible, into a comprehensible form like writing. Use Obsidian to store knowledge, connect, and retrieve stuff you learn. 

Practice communicating in other forms. Pictures, motions, expressions.
