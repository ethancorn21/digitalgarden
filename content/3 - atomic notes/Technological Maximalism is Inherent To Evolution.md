# Technological Maximalism is Inherent

The ancestors that didn't adopt the life changing technologies, like fire, were eventually dominated by those that did. So perhaps there's an inherent aspect of us that jumps on new technologies as a survival mechanism to thrive, ingrained in us from the first ancestor that picked up a sharp rock and took a swing. 

We are tool using creatures. We judge the newest tools, essential for our survival and thriving, on their utility. So then, my hypothesis for our technological maximalism especially for artificial intelligence, is based on this evolutionary lock-step.

We as a society are generally apprehensive of where artificial intelligence is taking us, yet we are building the very important pillars of the future based on these uncertain technologies. I am not arguing for favorability in any use case or disuse case of artificial intelligence, but I do find it interesting how we are apprehensively mass adopting this new technology.

History repeats itself. With the uncertainty that is perpetuated by artificial intelligence, it's not hard to imagine that our ancestors felt the same when we utilized the atom, created the first computers, calculators, stirrup, written language, fire, stone tool, on and on. When the status quo is vulnerable, we feel an inevitable pull to resist the ambiguously changed world.
