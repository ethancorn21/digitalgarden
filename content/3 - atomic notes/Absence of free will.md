# Absence of free will

because of the way we learn, we draw from the past to react to the present. With a similar mechanism, we also try to predict the future from events of the past. So our present and future decisions are made based off of a fixed past. Would it not be reasonable to say that our decisions are also fixed since the time we have experienced the past that would eventually lead to our present decisions? 

The idea of causal chains of cause and effect since the beginning of the universe states that every single action that has ever taken place and will take place must have a previous action that causes it. So me typing this is the result of the unfolding of the causal chains set in place since the beginning of the universe.

So then are we just reactive beings, locked into how we will act because the past? What are the arguments against predeterminism (not from a Christian perspective)?
