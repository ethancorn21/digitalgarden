# The 5 S's to Eliminate Waste

Sort / segregate - spend beginning of day sorting tools. Never spend time searching

Simplify / Straighten - method analysis. Are my tools effective and efficient?

Clean / Sweep - clean daily

Standardize - remove variations from processes.

Sustain / self discipline - review work. How can you improve it? Constant self reflection and analysis. You won't have the best method or process. Constant iteration.
