# Freeing Mental Bandwidth

Putting your thoughts and notes down into an external environment allows the brain to move onto the next thing with out wasting mental bandwidth on holding the information in the working memory.

What is the point of trying to remember that you have this important thing that must be done, when you can note it down in your calendar so that you do not have to keep it in your working memory.

The goal is to create a trusted system that allows you to put down things that need to happen or that you want to remember (tasks, birthdays, cool facts, etc.), and that it is reliable enough to bring it to your attention when you need it.

Calendar app, notes app, Anki. These are all you need to learn and organize yourself. Anything more has diminishing marginal utility.
