# Reinforcing World View

Echo chambers. We seek to reinforce the belief that we are on the side right, in turn protecting our perceived gain of future status within the group. 

If we view ourselves as always on the right side of some polarizing issue, our perceived future gain that will come with the spoils of being right is great. To reconcile that we might not be 100% correct, we group together with others who reinforce our world view and suppress doubt.

Take care to look past the left and right of today to realize that much of what you will do is in pursuit of some worldly gain, not necessarily moral wins.
