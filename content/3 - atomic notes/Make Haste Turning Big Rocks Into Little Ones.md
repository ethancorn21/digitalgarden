# Make Haste Turning Big Rocks Into Little Ones

Much of life will seem pointless. Endless repetition of congenial takes that are by all metrics not so bad, but they slowly reel you in under the weight of a combined arms of a million little pains that come from a life of tiny displeasures with no real struggle to speak of.

Camus points to the myth of Sisyphus as not so different to the endless, unrelenting pain that is life. He points to how we interpret Sisyphus, that if we see his tasks as meaningless, than we must see our own lives as such. But instead if we seek to find pleasure in the meaninglessness, then we regain a sort of control over our lives. One that allows us to make a good time of the short times we have in the mortal plane.
