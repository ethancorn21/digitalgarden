# Consciousness vs Intelligence

Intelligence is a purposeful act of doing. Consciousness is a state of being. Animals are a thing, they exists. But do they exists in a conscious state, separate from that which seems to just exists within any soft tissue that controls action, which we deem a brain and which produces intelligence. Animals can exhibit intelligence by purposefully doing. 

But where does the line from intelligence cross into consciousness? We like to gravitate toward the idea that consciousness stems from an intelligence that exists within the human brain. 

This opens the door for Artificial Intelligence. Already, any frontier LLM is smarter than any singular person alive in breadth of knowledge and smarter than the vast majority in depth of knowledge. But this does not mean that is consciousness. Does it even have the mechanistic prerequisites to achieve consciousness?
