# Importance of Preparation

When you have a 30 minute meeting, why would you not take a quick 5-10 minutes quickly preparing for it? This ensures that you know what you want to say, how the meeting should be paced, what needs to be gotten from the attendees. 

A quick couple minute sacrifice to save hours and hours of everyones time. Extremely asymmetric returns that yields little downside. 

Now, when you prepare very well and you know that speech or topic like the back of your hand, you can mix in improvisation. It gives you a hit of authenticity without the rigid nature that speeches often seem to have.

For example, when you were giving job interviews, think of the candidates who had their answer memorized. They would go on and on about the stuff they wanted to incorporate into their answers, yet they never seemed to realize that their answer was already sufficient and by continually talking they are only detracting from themselves. So if they had mixed in a bit better of improvisation and made themselves feel more comfortable and more natural, than I would not have noticed it.
