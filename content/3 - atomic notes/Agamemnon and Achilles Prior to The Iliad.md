# Agamemnon and Achilles Prior to The Iliad

![[Jacques-Louis_David_-_The_Anger_of_Achilles_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg|600]]

Long time beef. Agamemnon boasted that he was a better hunter than Apollo. In retaliation Apollo stopped the sea winds, trapping the Greeks at the port of Aulis. Unable to set sail to Troy, Agamemnon devised a scheme to sacrifice his daughter, Iphigenia, to appease Apollo. But first he had to lure his family to Aulis.

Thus he promised to marry her to the great hero Achilles. Both sides, excited about their prospects, agreed.

But upon arrival, Agamemnon took his daughter and began the ritual sacrifice. She would have been bound and gagged. Hung upside down, exposing her throat. And had her throat slit with ceremonial knife.

Older tales will have had Iphigenia die. But the story evolved to have Apollo swap her head out with that of a deer, faking her death, and taking her away to serve as a priestess.
