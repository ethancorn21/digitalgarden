# Appeal of the Peasant Mindset

We have this sort of peasant mindset where we are always looking for our next king to rule our lives. Whether it be through a religious deity or a materialistic item, we give up our selves in an effort to attain whatever it is we deify.

Why is it that we elevate things on a pedestal for us to worship and praise?

We are highly attuned to status and in that worship of something we can gain a sense of that status. By being the most zealous we can get reputation and status within the group. By having the nice watch or the cool car we can gain status as someone who has resources. By being the most fit or best looking we can show off our genetic superiority, thus gaining status. 

But what is all of this but an appeal to a inner entity acted upon by an outside force. Pleasing the ego by projecting value outwards in the hope that the projection is reflected back onto the self in the form of acknowledgement of the projection. We hope to self-soothe this intense feeling of needing to be apart of the tribe by signaling to the tribe that they need us. Then we look for signs of that signaling working. Caveman shit.
