# Fighting Entropy

Life is a battle to fight the natural entropy that occurs in all things. Rarely are things evergreen, instead most things are ever fleeting. Energy put into a system will dissipate and return to a baseline level of normalcy, usually a pre energetic injection state.

The goal is to constantly fight the entropic energy leakage inherent to a system / process. How can I maintain and combat the energy leakage inherent to everything when time is so finite? I can only think to simply and not diffuse my focus. I can increase the efficacy of the energy I put into a system by slimming down on the processes that require my energy. Given the chance, energy will leave a system faster than it was put into it. 

Stop putting effort into maintaining relationships and they will fall apart. Stop stressing your mind and it will forget and not operate at it's peak. Stop lifting and you will get weaker and smaller. Stop talking to people and you will become an antisocial weirdo. Stop self-iteration and you will never grow.
