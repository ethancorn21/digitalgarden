# Free Will as the Unfolding of Causal Links

 The universe is a causal entity. A series of reactions that has taken place since the beginning of the universe. If everything is a series of reactions to the last "thing", can we not say that in this universe everything happens, BECAUSE of a reason. So, since everything happens because of a reason and nothing truly happens on its own, we can then also say there are a series of causal links that has happened since the beginning of time which we can trace back to see what has caused what. For example: Ethan is an animal, animals get the energy their body needs to survive from the world around them, so Ethan gets hungry, making him get something to eat. This would be one minuscule causal chain. You can imagine that since the big bang, and even before it, there would have to be a cause for everything in the universe. There exists nothing in isolation.

Think about what that does to free will. Like a set of train tracks since the beginning of time itself, every action has led to this exact moment. Did you really have control over any of it? Can you truly say that you came to these conclusions as a result of your own agency and not the causal links that made you who you are and dictated that you would react that way.

You might define free will as your ability to make choice. But are these choices not based off of what has happened in past causal links? If these decisions are past off of past causal links and these links are unfolding as you act throughout your life, has your decisions and actions not already been determined by these past causal links? You will continue reading this because your past self chose to continue reading 10 seconds ago. Did you choose this or did they?
