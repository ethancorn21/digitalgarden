[[value creation]], [[mindful consumption]],[[analysis]], [[Inversion Philosophy]]

Value creation cannot be solely confined to revenue efficiency. There is a certain efficacy from sacrificing something that we want, to give users what they need. We see the outset of squeezing pennies out of consumers and we see the pushback from it. 

I am reminded of piracy advocates who will quote Gabe Newell at his famous Uni talk, "Piracy is almost always a service problem". Service problems almost always stem from features that are not created to be used, but instead to increase revenue or clout. 

True evolution of expertise is to eventually value simplicity. When you create you must ensure that the function of your creation does not change. Who are the ends users? What do they realistically need/want? It is not about pushing the latest tech or being flashy. It is about creating value and robustness for users. Be thinking of pure value add for the consumer.
