# The General Misunderstanding of Recovery

For some reason we see recovery as this passive activity. Whether physical or emotional recovery. Sit on the couch and drool while your brain is occupied so that you can't even think of the problem. Drink away the weekend, so you don't have to think of the stresses of the past and coming week. Even passively reading a book. These are all ways of running from the stress and problems at hand. A way to distract the brain with one hand while slowly lobotomizing it with the other.

If the stress is inescapable you must find ways to actively recover. A weekend of passive recovery is not enough time to recover from the intense stress of the week. That is why you must actively seek it out. Working out is active recovery for the mind. Walks where you can reach and sustain a meditative state are also forms of recovery for the mind and body. Turning your mind away from the stress of school and work towards projects in which you see yourself making progress and growing are also great. Learning about things you actually care about. Fraternizing with people you love, respect, and support. Even turning the spotlight away from the world and onto yourself and introspecting so that you may make progress in knowing thyself. These are all great ways to recover.
