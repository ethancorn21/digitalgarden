# Momentum

1 year drug, alcohol, social media free. The melting pot of a hermits life, and yet now I have more genuine connections, self-confidence and awareness, and more purpose in my life than ever before. 

Momentum carries you forward. When you better yourself in area of the self, the rest soon follows. Just as there are downward spirals, there exist upward ones as well. They begin just as the downward spirals do, with a choice.

Recognize that it’s always when you feel low and down that you feel drawn to the pull of escapism that drugs and social media bring. When you feel strongly about The Purpose, and invigorated by the work, then the rest of my life unfolds as if preordained.

Purpose allows you to shirk off the trivial struggles of life. The hedonic temptations of life become easy to forgo in favor of the grand purpose.
