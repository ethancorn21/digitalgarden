# Test Thoroughly

When creating anything your goal is to ensure that what you are creating is not a piece of shit. Find your edge cases. Find the limitations of what you created. Is there a better way to do things?

You can begin to find the answer by testing a lot. Do small tests of components. Do medium test of the multiple components. Do whole system tests by introducing chaos into your systems.
