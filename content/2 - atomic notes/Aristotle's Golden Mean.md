# Aristotle's Golden Mean

Aristotle posited that virtue is the middle ground between excess and deficiency.

For example, bravery is the middle ground between cowardice and carelessness.

A quick algorithm for answering "should I do X?":
- what is the most hedonic, self-serving decision?
- what is the most self-less, altruistic decision?
- where do the middle ground between the two lie?
