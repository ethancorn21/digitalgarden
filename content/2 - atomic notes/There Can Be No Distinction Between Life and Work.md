# There Can Be No Distinction Between Life and Work

We see life and work as separate. That if we do our 8 hours we can get on with what really makes us happy. That kind of thinking locks us out of getting pleasure from our work. From carrying on for the love of the game.

Why should I take on the perspective that my time at work is not my own? A shift in perspective is needed so that the daunting challenge of doing what must be done is not a grind against a wall, but a challenge to be taken up.

See your work as part of your life’s task. It is not simply to get a paycheck, but to advance in the skills that you need to achieve the end goal you set out for yourself. Motivated and energized by the work, that commute in won’t be so bad. That project won’t seem too boring. 

If your work does not align with your life’s purpose, look to skills that you gain from that job while you figure out your exit. Can you learn how to talk with others, to work under pressure, how you best organize yourself. There is always a way to move forward even in a dead end.
