# 4 M's of Manufacturing Defects

All manufacturing defects and inefficiencies are caused by:
- Methodology
- Materials
- Machines
- Manpower

If you wish to create / manage an organization, these are the 4 pillars of creating anything. Focus on them, continuously iterate and refine these.
