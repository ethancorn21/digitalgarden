# Contracts of Self-Criticism and Dissatisfaction

We enter into self-agreed contracts with ourselves in which we give ourselves permission to act in out of the ordinary ways in exchange for progress in pursuit of some other thing. Think of the self-agreed contracts that we enter into with ourselves. How many of them are positive or negative? 

Our goals are a sort of self-contract. We tell ourselves to aspire to something, and to get there we must sacrifice pieces of ourselves to that goal. Yet what are those goals but fleeting markers when accomplished. Goals that will always change and shift for what is a Human without a goal in the back of their mind?

Desire can be a self-contract. One that places pieces of ourselves inside an ever elusive object. One that can't be attained so we can never get those pieces back. A chase of an ever elusive desire.

Perhaps the most destructive contracts are those that combine the two. The desire to achieve goals. To become more. When we are constantly in a state of desire for a future state, we can never be in our current state.

A constant and perpetual state of future facing prospects, locking us into "what ifs" rather than "what is".
