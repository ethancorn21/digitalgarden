# The goal of social communication

How much of my social communication stems from a need from "I"? How often do I interject with a story that is relatable to no one other than myself? In an effort to be heard, I often forget to hear.

The goal of social communication is not to be heard, but to hear. You must make an effort to hear what the other person is trying to say, whether outright or implied. People give hints to what they care about in everything they do. Notice those hints, talk about what those hints imply, make them feel heard and seen. That is all anyone wants. For someone to say, "I hear you and I see you, you are not crazy".
