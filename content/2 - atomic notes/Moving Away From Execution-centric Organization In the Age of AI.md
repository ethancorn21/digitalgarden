# Moving Away From Execution-centric Organization In the Age of AI

Organizations revolve themselves around execution. Delivery of a plan on the planned date, of the planned quality, using the planned people. The goal of execution-centric organizations is to maximize throughput of business operations by standardizing execution. This is done through defined responsibility over the moving parts. The owner responsibility model of getting things done is largely due to how specialized knowledge workers must be. The accountant doesn't understand the network and vice versa. 

But knowledge is seemingly becoming a commodity before a certain skill threshold is met. Associates and juniors are often out skilled by the knowledge and capabilities of LLMs. But it is of my opinion that poor systems integrations often stop this ability to be fully unleashed.

Even senior members are outclassed in terms of raw knowledge. But that is not a know, as there is no human alive on Earth that can speak in depth and as broadly across domains as a modern frontier LLM. Now where does the perceived stupidity that really holds LLMs back come from then?

Slop is what we call the output of LLMs and the slop churners who create it need to understand context and laziness. There is also many tokens that a LLM can intake within a session before it needs to start a new session. This is called your context window, it is the LLMs working memory. To preserve this relatively short context window (~1 mil tokens for Opus level models), LLMs take shortcuts. That is the root of the slop. Instead of refactoring, an LLM simply bolts on parts.

Rapid implementation should now be the default operating methodology for orgs. Combined with canary testing, you can test new user features in a matter of minutes. Why should we wait for scrum teams to storyboard BS when we can get multiple experimentation iterations deep by the time that they come to finish their brain games. 

When every product team can experiment on their product with maximal efficiency, the valued abstraction layer moves one step up.
