# Moving Away From Execution-centric Organization In the Age of AI

Organizations revolve themselves around execution. Delivery of a plan on the planned date, of the planned quality, using the planned people. The goal of execution-centric organizations is to maximize throughput of business operations by standardizing execution.

Rapid implementation should now be the default operating methodology for orgs. Combined with canary testing, you can test new user features in a matter of minutes. Why should we wait for scrum teams to storyboard BS when we can get multiple experimentation iterations deep by the time that they come to finish their brain games. 

When every product team can experiment on their product with maximal efficiency, the valued abstraction layer moves one step up.
