# High-Availability (HA)

Ensures performance for an agreed upon level of operational performance, usually uptime, for a higher than normal period.

Does not mean that customers will never experience outages or failures.

HA systems are designed so that when it fails, its components are able to be quickly repaired or fixed so that it can come back up faster. HA often uses automation for fixes. HA is not about UX, its about maximizing online time / minimizing downtime.

Sometimes standby servers / infrastructure is needed to cut over to in the event of an outage.
