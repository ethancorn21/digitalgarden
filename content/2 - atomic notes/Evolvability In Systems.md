# Evolvability In Systems

Evolvability in a system references it's ability to change and adapt to the changing needs of those that are within the system. As time goes on the purpose that a system serves will have changed. And so that system needs to adapt in order to remain relevant for the stakeholders of that system. 

Evolvability at it's core is about agility. Not being swamped down with the current, but instead being able to enact the change that the future demands.

One creates agility through simplicity, portability, and maintainability. The system must be simple enough to understand. It must be portable to a new form factor. It must be maintainable for the foreseeable future. They all benefit from thoughtful design and architecture, as well as useful but not limiting abstractions of the system.
