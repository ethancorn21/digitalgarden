# Unconscious Processing

We get "aha!" moments seemingly out of nowhere. When brushing our teeth, showering, sleeping, etc. Pretty much any idle activities except TV and scrolling social media can induce this state. Allowing your mind to idle and wander on its own is great because it allows your non conscious mind to compute things that you are not aware of. 

In the background of your mental forefront is an entire show running behind the scenes. What you are conscious of is only a drop in an ocean of what is truly going on inside your head. As you go about your day, the problems that you are stuck on are being calculated and reasoned through by your unconscious.

Take advantage of this by offloading mental bandwidth to your unconsciousness. Purposefully stimulate unconscious processing by reaching meditative states of physical absorption so that your mental form separates from physical reality around it. Things like sensory deprivation work well here as evidenced by shower thoughts.

The key is to partake in activities that allow your mind to disengage from what's in front of it so that it may begin the computations in the back of the mind.
