# Socio Christian Movement

There has been a dramatic increase in outspoken Christian efforts to repatriate an increasingly atheistic world. Think of how many people you know that proudly put their faith on display. Is this not the direct opposite of what sociological statistics are saying? Of course the younger generation is more conservative compared to their millennial peers, but this is to be expected within a political pendulum. But what can we say about the Gen X and Millennials that are also subscribing to this new Christian fervor. 

The social christian movement is a backlash against the spread of liberal ideals and Islam into Western society. Two very clashing ideals, but ones easy to make a boogie man.

Look at the conversion of Ayaan Hirsi Ali, a hugely influential proponent of atheism. She would do talks with Richard Dawkins, Christopher Hitchens, Sam Harris, Daniel Dennett. These guys are literally THE atheists. Actual poster child of Atheism, and Ali would go a do tours with these people talking about how much she wanted global atheism. And yet...

Today she is a converted Christian. Her reason? She believes Christianity is a bulwark against Islam.

The social christian movement will use Christianity as the foundation of a more conservative culture. The glue to bind a civilization together.

Is it warranted? People are apt at justifying their immorality, so I think that some sort of uncompromising moral framework combined with an inherent sense of direction is good for society as a whole. I mean we tried science, shit didn't work. It just gave rise to a society of Redditors and weirdly socialized Millennials that seem to be adult-babies.
