[[process]] ; [[value creation]] ; [[Inversion Philosophy]]


The goal is to have a supreme focus on value provided. This means emphasizing the quality of the few over the quantity of the many. Allowing opportunity for degradation of quality, ripples down from bottom to top. 

Individuals focusing on improving their processes affects the value chain such that the organization reaps the rewards. So if we can create a culture of process improvement through experimentation and knowledge discovery, then we have effectively created a self-regulating organization that will find the most efficient path towards a shared end goal.
