# Intellectual Ego

Be careful hurting people’s intellectual ego. It’s one of the things they can’t let slide. Most people anyways. These people will often show themselves in little ways during your interactions. Say you are working on something and rephrase what they said to gauge if you understood what they meant. If they said something defensive like, “that’s what i said”, or “yeah that’s literally what i was talking about”. They feel the need to have their ideas contributed to them. They have to have others know that they idea floating around the psychosphere is theirs.

When dealing with these people. No problem, let ideas be theirs. Draw attention to it when they do contribute a good idea. The goal is to soothe the ego, not inflame them and get protracted into an intellectual pissing match, not beneficial for you. IT has a way higher concentration of people this way, actual neckbeards.

For you to not make the same mistake, you must not gain any satisfaction in others thinking you are smart. Recognize that you see but a glimpse and know nothing.
