# Retreating Into Passivity

People, when overwhelmed, will revert to a passive state as a form of rectifying control over the overwhelming situation. When we feel as though a particular situation has spiraled out of control we will attempt to regain control in our minds by changing our attitude toward the situation. 

Instead of being active, we can be passive and let it consume what it will naturally consume. Though we lost control over the fire, we exercised control over the self.

But of course think of the mindset this infects one with. "If I don't try in life, if I limit what I care about, I can never say that I have failed".
An attempt of creating a self-illusion. Make it seem as though you are not responsible for what happens to you, and the circumstances of your inaction are easier to cope with.

Fate ceases to be a product of our own hands and becomes one of chance, Genetics, luck. It's an self-soothing narrative to attempt to wrest control by coping with perceived powerlessness of one's life.
