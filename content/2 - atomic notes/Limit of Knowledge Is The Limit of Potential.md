# Limit of Knowledge Is The Limit of Potential
![[Heinrich_fueger_1817_prometheus_brings_fire_to_mankind.jpg|300]]
>The limits of my language mean the limits of my world.
>\- Ludwig Wittgenstein, *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus*

How well you understand the world and others is a direct result of the language you need to process that understanding. The more words you know, the more granular you can get with your thoughts. The more concepts and models you have at your disposal, the less surprising new information is. Your ability to express yourself works in conjunction with the limits of your knowledge. 

Seek to expand the world in which you see. New language, new mental models, concepts from a wide variety of subjects. The more broadly you can learn, understand, then apply will broaden the limits of your world, as Wittgenstein implied.

Do you think that so many of the greatest people throughout history coincidentally praise the learned life and examined life? Why forgo the model provided and expounded upon by millions of people throughout history and provided for you. 

Light a fire under yourself with purpose, learn as a vehicle for better understanding your inner and outer world. People are counting on you to know these things, to have the mental models in place to be resilient and adaptive enough to solve the problems of the day.
