# Problems with the Nonchalant

Trying, being enthusiastic about what you were put onto this earth to do is one of the most positive actions you can commit in this world. Finding purpose and projecting that into the world brings forth a better world. It is inaction that causes destruction.

We live in an entropic universe. Energy not in use will wither and dissipate. So, in order to realize the institutions, technology, and orders that need to occur in order for us to thrive as humans, we must take action: the direct antithesis to not caring. Every minute we spend transforming the zeitgeist into non-caring, passive dickheads we face enormous opportunity cost of future greats.

You find that the older you get the nonchalant shit is stupid. Not caring is to be passive. Passive inaction in a world full of problems is destructive selfishness.

Effort is cool. Caring is cool. Trying is cool.
