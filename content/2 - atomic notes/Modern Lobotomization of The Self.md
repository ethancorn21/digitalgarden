# Modern Lobotomization of The Self

Does it not seem that we are unconsciously lobotomizing ourselves so that we no longer hurt?

A modern life is the empty shell of what we are biologically evolved to do. We are gorillas inside a concrete pit at the zoo that we put ourselves into.

There is a silent hurt that living causes. A dull ache at the back of the mind that signifies you are living and that it is a ever fleeting finite experience. What is finite is not being spent in congruence with that fact. Instead one's life is being lived as if there were a variety of new starts to choose from after this life ends.

We have to go out and purposefully seek the stimulation that our brain was meant for, all the while dodging the silicon valley experiments that seem to be able to so accurately hijack our primitive brain.

It's no wonder that it feels good to escape and distract oneself in congruence with the denial of death and the fact that we are doing nothing worthy of this infinitesimally unique experience. So if it's all going to be a waste anyway, why live in reality where your problems lie and cause constant stress when you can live in chemically altered and virtual realities? Because that is the easy thing to do. The easy choice to make.

By choosing the "easy" way out of distraction, we are lobotomizing ourselves to the pain of life. It feels as if we are subconsciously affectionate for the reality where we are fully lobotomized, drained, and willingly ignorant of the shit disposition that we find ourselves in. 

So, don't allow yourself to fall slave to the easy path. The path that allows for life to disappear past you, until you awake one day to the realization, "This is it?"
Recognize that when you find yourself drawn to substances and distractions, that when you feel like shit and don't want to push on, you must make for yourself a goal to strive for. To give yourself purpose that gives you the option to say, "I am solely focused on my goal, nothing else".
