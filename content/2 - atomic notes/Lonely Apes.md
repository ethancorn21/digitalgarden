# Lonely Apes

We are but lonely apes. Hoping someone reaches their hands out, grabs us, and takes us with them. We only want to find our tribe, our belonging. For social creatures, to be tribe-less is to face certain death. Our intense fear of loneliness, FOMO, insecurities. They stem from our ancestors developing these psychological mechanisms to survive. To not be kicked out of the tribe. It's primitive ape shit all the way down as far as Humans psychology is concerned.

How much of what we do stems from an unconscious instinct to be a part of a social mass rather than apart from it. When you observe what others and especially yourself, recognize this need.
