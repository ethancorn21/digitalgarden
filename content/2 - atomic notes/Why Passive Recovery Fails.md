# Why Passive Recovery Fails

We tend to project a hedonistic aspect onto recovery, more specifically [[The General Misunderstanding of Recovery|passive recovery]]. This type of recovery consists of scrolling, drinking, the passive pleasure seeking activities that we turn around and say is recovery. 

But it is not sustainable by itself. Over time the pleasure we gain from these types of activities will falter because of the [[Hedonic Pleasure Treadmill]]. As such the recovery factor caused by the enjoyment we received from said activity also falters. Over time we come to the realization that our current mechanisms of rest no longer work, leaving us even more exhausted for the journey ahead.

That is why we must learn from the Greeks and seek to balance [[Eudaimonia]] and [[Hedonia]]. The pleasurable and the purposeful. It is through this balance of the two that we can arrive at periods of recovery that invigorate and relax us.
