# Connections are the Point

Connections are the point of life. 

If we look to the Social Intelligence Hypothesis, the reason we are conscious, the reason our brain are so powerful is because the needs that being a social creature creates. We had to keep track of complex relationships and social dynamics of a 20 - 60 person tribe. According to the theory, everything the brain is was in part evolved to handle complex social interactions. It would explain why connections feels so good.

Being apart of a community leads one to have a positive Halo Effect over the rest of one's life. It also fills a instinctual predisposition to group together to better one's survival chance.

In your research of deathbed confessions and regrets, has anyone ever said that they wished they didn't cultivate strong relationships? To me, it just seems as though it's hard to be regretful about. Of course, this is different from letting FOMO cause constantly diverge away from the self in adherence to the desire to be apart of the group.

Family, friends, loved ones, community. How much of what you do is in pursuit of those predisposed goals set by your ancestors survival? Is it realistic or even necessary to try and push back against it?
