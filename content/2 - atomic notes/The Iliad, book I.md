# The Iliad, book I

![[Jacopo_Alessandro_Calvi_-_Chryses_Vainly_Soliciting_the_Return_of_Chryseis_before_the_Tent_of_Agamemnon.jpg|600]]

In the ninth year of the Siege of Troy, the Iliad begins. Agamemnon, King of Mycenae (the most powerful Greek city-state at the time), commander of the entire Greek expeditionary force, divides the spoils of battle amongst his men. His grand prize was the daughter of an Apollonian priest. This priest came with a large ransom to Agamemnon to plead for his daughter release. Against the advice of his war council, Agamemnon refused. Hurt, the priest prayed to his patron, Apollo. Apollo unleashed a plagues upon Agamemnon's men. 

In order to release the Plague, Agamemnon accepted the ransom of the Priest. But not satisfied at not having his spoils, Agamemnon forcefully takes Achilles, Prince of Pthia and leader of the Myrmidons, war spoils. A woman name Briseis. Achilles, a hot head, was naturally outraged at this upset. Such that he refused to help Agamemnon's conquest of Troy anymore. Of course Achilles was Agamemnon's greatest warrior. So this caused a good bit of outrage by the other leaders of Agamemnon's armies 

Achilles, a demigod,  summoned his mother, Thesia, a river nymph. He told her was happened and asked that she ask Zeus to help Agamemnon enemies, The trojans, Thesia agreed and waited for Zeus in Olympus. Zeus agreed to help her, secretly though because Hera, His wife was already angry at him over his suspected helping of the Trojans. 

Zeus promises Thesia that he will help Agamemnon's enemies, the Trojans.
