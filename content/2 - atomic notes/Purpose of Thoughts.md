# Purpose of Thoughts

We can crank out as many thoughts as we want, but what's the point if they are not leading to combined weight grander than the might of the individual mind.

A bricklayer does not lay the brick to say he laid mud and clay, he does it to erect a structure. A structure that takes it place among other structures to form towns and cities.

Stone mason work with massive blocks of dense stone to fulfill grand architectural plans that will stand for centuries as an ideal to strive for. A building that serves as a paragon of beauty and craftsmanship. Craftsmanship that other stonemasons strive for. Being the stonemason is harder, blocks are larger, weights are measured in tons per block. But your contribution is more substantive, more permanent.

Our thoughts should be building our brain to an ideal that we strive toward. Constant implementation of a self referential loop that continually actualizes latent potential into momentum towards a higher state of being.
