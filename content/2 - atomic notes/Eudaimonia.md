# Eudaimonia

Greek concept for "human flourishing". They believed that one achieved eudaimonia when one has a deep life purpose and strives to achieve that purpose. Greeks saw it as a source of life happiness.

Aristotle believed that trying to achieve eudaimonia is the ultimate meaning of life. That if we were virtuous is reason, compassion, and courageous that we might come close to achieving it.
