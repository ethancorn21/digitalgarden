# Proxy Metrics Are Bad Measurements

Proxy metrics are only a sign of local optimization. Using number such as lines of code, tickets closed, frequency of deployment are only localized functions of a department. They are NOT DIRECTLY linking to the businesses needed outcomes, like revenue.

The problem with IT nerds is that they don't get this part. Cost centers are perma-beggars, and yet find ways to want to be choosers. Defining your work in terms of proxy metrics is cool for inter-departmental stuff. But if you are talking business and pull out your ticket count, you deserve what's coming to you.
