# Protestantism as a Gateway to Capitalism

Protestants like Calvinist believe in pre-determinism, that their salvation is already decided. So some Calvinist have been preordained as saved, they look to worldly acquisitions as evidence of God's judgement. Things like job promotions, material acquisitions are looked to as signs of God's favor.

This protestant mindset led to a reshaping of the collective consciousness. Reinvestment of Protestant funds that were acquired through labor led to growth, further encouraging a capitalistic mindset.
