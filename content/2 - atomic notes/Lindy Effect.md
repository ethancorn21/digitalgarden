# Lindy Effect

The longer something has survived thus far, the longer it will likely survive. 

This effectively summarizes trends and the zeitgeist. What we find as people: we cannot help but succumb to trends in some shape. But trends are not survivable. What is cool today is cringe in 10 years.

The goal then is to escape the tides of the culture and follow what has been preached for hundreds and thousands of years. The conclusions that people have come to after thousands of hours of deliberation, thought, and scrutiny.

In the race for swag, authenticity will always win out. Fuck it, i'm gonna start wearing a cape.
