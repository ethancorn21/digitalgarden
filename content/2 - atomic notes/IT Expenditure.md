# IT Expenditure

Companies will only fund IT projects that lower costs, increase performance or capacity, increase reliability.

Show how your idea saves money. Things like recurring expenses, maintenance costs, consulting fees, equipment costs, human resources costs (ie "if I implement this, it will save us X amount in staffing costs, allowing us to recoup investment within 2 years).

Show how your ideas increase performance. Will your idea provide leverage for those who use it? For example, if you wanted to propose a new network change, could this network change possibly speed up user's work?

Reliability: Can this change make maintenance easier, availability time higher, and negate downtime risk?
