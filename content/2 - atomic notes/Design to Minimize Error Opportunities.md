# Design to Minimize Error Opportunities

Design your systems to minimize the opportunities for errors, more specifically Human errors. The best software in the world is meaningless if it is prone to causing human errors. 

Design abstractions that are easy to use and powerful enough for everyday use, but can be more granular if the power is needed. Be especially prudent at handicapping people in an effort to abstract away too much.

I am reminded of the point and say method. The Tokyo subway authorities were noticing an issue where safety inspections that occurred at every platform were leaving some issues to slip between the gaps. Through no fault of the inspectors, their brains had developed a heuristic over thousands and thousands of inspection to the point where they were only looking for those quick heuristics. But by introducing the point and say technique, where inspectors would point to their inspection point and say out loud, "X piece is good because of Y", their error rates dropped substantially.
