# Having LLM's Create Steelman Arguments Against Your Work
Use technology to strengthen yourself. The arguments you make are especially receptive to this form of AI leveraged thinking.

Ask an LLM:
- List the 5 questions this audience is most likely to ask, hardest first.
- For each, tell me whether my current material answers it or not.
- Flag the single weakest claim I am making and how someone would attack it.
- Give me one number or piece of context I should have ready that I probably left out.
- identify what assumptions I'm making versus what I've actually proven.
- deliver a comprehensive steelman case against the exhibit
