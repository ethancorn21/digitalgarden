# If You Can't Decide Then Be Fast

If something is ambiguous, there isn't a clear way forward. Act quickly so that you are first. If you can't be right, be first.

Rarely are decision truly irreversible. Even the ones that are thought to be irreversible, have a delta of the initial value of investment/payment and the amount that can be recouped. So don't take the heuristic of decision being 1's and 0's. Often times they more so look like: Initial cost - cost that can't be recuperated through reversal = actual cost of a decision.
