# Sacrifice Now To Escape Shifting Tides of Our Times

You have to sacrifice now if you wish to shield yourself from the social upheaval that will follow the technological leap in our times. Artificial Intelligence changes the game. You must learn how to efficiently harness, direct, and multiply your work through them. The skills that will run the world are not simply being a subject matter expert or even architectural design work. 

Do not fall back on the idea that we will be relegated to architectural work or managerial work. Quickly are we seeing the lines being erased from the sand. In my experiments with Opus 5.5 xHigh, it can do the architectural work. People who say otherwise are not looking beyond their current abilities to recognize that the pace between LLM abilities and there own is shrinking faster than they can continue to learn. 

LLM's don't need to be better than you they just need to be a cost-benefit threshold for you to be replaced. They don't need to rival John Carmack, they need with combined effort to do more, meet a certain quality threshold, and be cheaper than Mr.Carmack.

The tides of society are shifting fast, what worked 5 years ago will not work today. You can't allow yourself to follow the traditional guidelines for what worked. Look around, and use that as a litmus test for what average is. It is shockingly low. There are people who refuse to use AI to complement their work. There are others who rely wholly on it. You must find yourself in the middle. A force multiplier, but not a crutch. 

So what is the skills that will prosper?
