# Why We Suck at Estimating Time

When we have to estimate the time to do something, we more often than not suck at it. We tend to grossly underestimate the amount of time it takes to do something, even if it's something we have done time and time again. 

On first thought, it could it be that the pressure to achieve a specific goal by a set deadline pushes us to make optimistic estimations. But, if this is true, then why do we still make terrible guesses for time allotment even when we are asked for the worst case scenario?

The problem lies inherently in the act of estimating time. We are converting from one measurement to another unit of measurement that is not cross compatible. And it is in this conversion that we often lose the plot. 

For example say you have a spreadsheet that needs to be done. You have a bunch of microtasks to complete inside the spreadsheet for it to be deemed finished. We then take these microtasks, estimate how long each will take and then add up all the time estimations for the microtasks to get how long the spreadsheet will take. Do you see the inherent problem there?

We are taking one unit of measurement-work that needs to be done- and converting it to another unit of measurement, time. These two units of measurement have nothing to do with each other though. Sure, it takes time to do work, but it doesn't take work to do time. They aren't cross compatible with each other. 

How many breaths would it take you to count to 100? How many hours does it take to do one unit of work? Do you see how similar both of these questions are and yet one would be scoffed at and the other is considered a perfectly valid question. Of course we can never get rid of asking, "how long?" but that not the point. The point is to recognize that time estimation will be wrong a majority of the time.

When you are given or are giving a time estimation, take your initial time and double it. This will be far more accurate than your knee jerk response. If someone tells you it will take 30 minutes, expect an hour. If you want to tell someone it takes 15 minutes, tell them it will take 30.
