# High-Availability (HA) vs Fault Tolerance (FT)

Just because a system is highly available does not make it tolerant to faults. A high available system can go down if something fails, its the event of quickly having it come back up that makes it HA.

But, a fault tolerant system should be extremely resistant to going down. Where one failure can cause a HA system to go down and it will remain HA, one failure causing a FT system to go down means it loses it's fault tolerant status.
