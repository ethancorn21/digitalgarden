# Work Ethic is The Universal Currency of Respect

Hard work is hard. Nobody wants to do it. 

Yet, even if people are divided by culture, time, and technology, hard work is respected throughout history. We as Humans mythicize the people that faced impossible odds and yet accomplished things so great that they echo in history as a permanent part of the Human canon. 

The ability to work hard and complete difficult tasks will always be respected. Yet, there exists a temperament that hard work is rewarded with more work. Of course there is always nuance, but what is that work but opportunities to sharpen yourself and your abilities. When you care about your work, your craft, you are almost compelled to strive for greatness in it. That can only be achieved through reps and striving.
