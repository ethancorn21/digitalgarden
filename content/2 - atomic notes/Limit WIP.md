# Limit WIP

Limit your work in progress to an ideal of one per batch. By limiting WIP you can see what are the potential roadblocks of future completion. 

Look at it like this. Why can't you do a batch size of one? Is it because you are waiting for something to complete, and in that meantime you feel the need to start another batch? In that case, why not work to minimize that downtime delay. 

By eliminating the delay, you focus solely on the bottleneck of your work. Experiencing a process improvement that improves your throughput. 

The common idea would be to do a bunch of things one step at a time. So do step 1 to 10 items, then step 2 to 10 items, etc. It seems counter-productive that step 1, then step 2, then step 3, repeat on new item is the better way to go.
