# Group think

![[not-to-be-reproduced-1937(1).jpg!Large.jpg|400]]

Group Think is the collective turning off of the feedback loops that allow humans to persist through differing opinions and differing resources. By having a feedback loop, such as the collective consciousness focusing on one thing the only way forward is the one idea, the one shared idea of the group.

In a normal healthy group that is resistant to group think the feedback loop that stops group think from forming are the dissenters of that group. Differing opinions that stop the stampeding herd of thought in one direction, to suggest a differing one.

Group think is affected by many factors. Think: power dynamics in a group, difficulty of a subject, politics, members of the group's disposition to meekness, etc. 

I have most soon group think affect groups where the power dynamic of the top person consists of a blatant superiority over the group. Unquestioning towards the top's ideals, the group meekly goes along with the herd. Trusting that because the top's air of confidence in their reasoning is convincing enough such that it drowns out self-doubt of incorrectness in the rest of the group.
