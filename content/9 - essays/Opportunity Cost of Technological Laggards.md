# Opportunity Cost of Technological Laggards

![[IMG_4364.JPG|0]]

Our ancestors that didn't adopt the life changing technologies, like fire, were eventually dominated by those that did. We evolve traits that push us to instinctively jump on new technologies as a survival mechanism to thrive, ingrained in all of us from the first ancestor that picked up a sharp rock and took a swing. Concerning those past humans that refused to use the newest tools, the latest culture advancements, agriculture. What do you think happened to their culture, their moral worldview, their history? 

Being a technological decelerationist, brings no benefits. The people that are revered are the people that pushed the frontier. History does not remember the people that hedge their bets. The mechanics of inaction will over a long enough time horizon, be outshined by those of action. The mechanics of action force breakthrough, they force new horizons and frontiers to be charted. The opportunity cost of passivity means that while some are busy shaking their fist at the changing tides of the world, others are actualizing their vision for the future by utilizing the technology. So over generations, an automatic systematic deletion happens.

Those that refuse to adapt to new technology will be disadvantaged, compared to those that are early adopters and actively utilizing the technology to actualize their vision. They’re doing more than the laggards and late adopters and so they will always be able to outcompete. The funding and social clout needed to affect the zeitgeist are magnetically attracted to those that will wield technology. The inability or non-desire to adopt the technology signs a blood contract to getting left behind. Moral view's fades from existence because technology that you refuse to adopt is being adopted by others, and they are not only outproducing in terms of productivity but cultural sway in world views as well.

Those people throughout time who refused to be technological maximalist were forgotten. Etched into the geologic strata as artifacts of a bygone age. They did not adapt and so everything they valued, withered. There lies the easy lesson.

If you do have a moral qualm against the new system/technologies, you need to find a way to shift how you apply your leverage. Aim not to reverse the tides of change, but to bring about a world more consistent with your own view.

Of course, I do not see this with Anti-AI people. They do not wish to utilize the technology at all so they use third party institutions to bring about their change. For example, the call to halt the training of more intelligence frontier AI models by the US political machine. It's all so short sighted. We are seeing the formation and growth of a technology that will allow us to completely democratize knowledge. Push bio-technology to new extremes. Cure all ailments. Create the newest pleasures and experiences.

We’re always moving towards places of higher leverage where we can do more at a higher quality with less effort and so knowing that principle, of course AI is inevitable. We’re not going to magically go back to a pre-AI world we’re only ever going to get more entrenched in it. We will only ever utilize more AI with smarter models, at cheaper prices, with better integration within our systems so that we can always keep doing more work at a higher quality with less. Working in congruence with your vision to bring forth alternate technologies is the hard thing to do but it is strictly necessary depending upon your beliefs.

Stopping or even slowing AI research is not the universal answer. How often we find hitting the gas allows us to stay in motion, affected by physical forces that we are already familiar with, and can react accordingly. Rather than slamming the brakes and being forced to ingest and dissipate our own inertia and new unfamiliar forces. Figure out what you wish to champion and use the tools to champion your cause.
