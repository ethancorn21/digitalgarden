Retailers - purchase products from manufacturers / wholesalers to sell to customers.

Wholesalers are not retailers because wholesalers sell to other businesses, not individual consumers.

Level of difficulty a manufacturer experiences when trying to get retailers to carry their product is based on how vertically integrated the channel is.

3 more P's in addition to the 4 P's.
1) product
2) price
3) place
4) promotion
5) presentation
6) personnel
7) process

Omnichannel operations will use an integrated CRM (Customer Relationship management ) system to better store and handle customer relations related things.
